In a near-future Tokyo, Dr. Yuki Tanaka runs an underground clinic where she illegally edits people's memories. She can remove trauma, implant skills, even rewrite entire relationships. Business is booming until a mysterious client named Victor hires her to erase his memory of a crime he's about to commit—a crime that will happen tomorrow.

When Yuki refuses, Victor reveals he's a corporate enforcer for NeuroSync, the megacorporation that banned memory editing after weaponizing it for mass control. He's been ordered to eliminate Yuki and destroy all evidence of independent memory tech. But Victor is conflicted—his own daughter was saved by an underground memory editor years ago, and he's haunted by the orders he's forced to follow.

Yuki's former mentor, Dr. Chen, built the memory technology but disappeared after NeuroSync stole his research. Chen now lives in hiding, paranoid and broken, but he's the only one who knows how to create a "memory vaccine"—a way to make minds immune to NeuroSync's control. Yuki must find Chen, convince him to help, and stay ahead of Victor's hunt.

Her only ally is Kai, a street-smart info broker with perfect memory—a rare genetic condition that makes him immune to memory editing but also means he remembers every trauma, every loss, every painful moment of his life. He helps Yuki because he wants what she has: the ability to forget.

The story races toward a confrontation at NeuroSync's headquarters, where Yuki must choose between destroying the technology entirely—including her ability to help people—or refining it to free humanity from corporate control, knowing it could just as easily be weaponized again.
